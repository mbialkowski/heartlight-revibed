# 6. History

* **Atari 8-bit original (1990/91).** *Heartlight* was created by **Janusz Pelc**, the Polish
  author of *Robbo*, for the Atari XL/XE as a variation on *Boulder Dash*. Sources disagree on
  the year (1990 or 1991). Pelc had co-founded the publisher LK Avalon in 1989 and left it in
  1990.
* **Xland (1992).** Pelc co-founded the studio **xLand** in 1992. The resource files of the PC
  version are dated 1992 (sprites December 1992, sounds and music September–November 1992, the
  font April 1993), so the PC engine was written in 1992–93.
* **Heartlight PC (1994).** The MS-DOS version was published in 1994 by **Epic MegaGames**
  together with two other games by Pelc, *The Adventures of Robbo* and *Electro Man*, in the
  mail-order *Epic Puzzle Pack*. The levels of the PC version are by Janusz Pelc, Marek Kubowicz,
  Maciej Janiec, Maciej Mach, Marek Kubas and Jacek Aleksandrow; level 70 spells "EPIC" and
  level 43 "CHAOS" (also the name on the start-up picture).
* **Creative Commons release (2006).** Maciej Miąsik, co-author of the PC version, recompiled the
  game from the original sources (building it in DOSBox) and released it as freeware under
  **CC BY-SA 2.5**, with the permission of the copyright holders. Epic Games was not involved.
  The executable's banner says *"Heartlight PC Creative Commons BY-SA 2.5 release 2006 —
  Originally published in 1994 by Epic MegaGames and xland"*; `loading.ted` in the resources
  is dated 1999, from a later rebuild of the tools. This repository documents that 2006 release.
* **Later.** A browser remake with updated graphics appeared in 2020; the Pelc games have also
  been sold digitally (e.g. on GOG).
